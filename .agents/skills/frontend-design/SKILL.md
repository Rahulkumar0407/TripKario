# Frontend Design & Art Direction Skill

## 1. Core Principles: Anti-Generic Art Direction
- **No Template Syndrome**: Never settle for the formula of "centered heading + 3 rounded dark cards + orange pill button + stock photo."
- **Story-Driven Composition**: Structure long-form pages as a sequence of cinematic scenes rather than isolated rectangular component blocks.
- **Visual Weight & Hierarchy**: Establish clear dominant focal points in every viewport (Hero scale 100vh+, asymmetrical editorial collages, dominant active cards in carousels).
- **Physical Materiality**: Glass must have realistic optical traits (backdrop blur, dynamic edge refraction, saturation boost, distinct ambient vs contact shadows) rather than flat opacity.

## 2. Typography Mastery
- **Upright Authority**: Zero italics anywhere. Use serif for emotional prestige and clean sans-serif/mono for functional metadata.
- **Scale Contrast**: Create dramatic scale contrasts (e.g., 5rem-8rem headings paired with 0.75rem tracking monospace tags).
- **Masked Reveals & Staggers**: Reveal typography with mask clips or staggered line transitions rather than generic fade-up animations.

## 3. First-Class Dual Themes
- **Light Theme**: Sunlight + warm textured paper (`#F4EFE5`) + white translucent glass + warm shadows. Never a stark sterile white fallback.
- **Dark Theme**: Midnight film (`#0B0A09`) + warm amber/sandstone reflections + deep translucent glass. Never generic SaaS blue/purple.
